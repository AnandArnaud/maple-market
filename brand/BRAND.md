# Maple Market brand

Maple Market sells everyday objects made to last. The brand is warm, quiet and a little rustic.

## Colours

| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Maple (primary, buttons, accents) | `#B5473B` |
| Bark (headings, body text) | `#2B2520` |
| Oat (page background) | `#FAF7F2` |
| Cream (cards, surfaces) | `#FFFDF9` |
| Sand (borders, dividers) | `#E6DFD4` |
| Moss (secondary accent, used sparingly) | `#5C6E54` |

Light backgrounds, dark text. Maple is the only saturated colour; use it once per composition.

## Type

Headings: a geometric sans, tight letter spacing (we use the system sans in the app).
Body: the same family at a comfortable size. Prices are always set in the body colour, never in Maple.

## Logo

`logo.svg` is a rounded Maple square with a leaf notch. Minimum size 24 px. Keep clear space of
half the mark's width around it. Do not recolour it.

## Voice

Plain, specific, unhurried. Name the material and the maker. No exclamation marks, no superlatives.

## Social

Square posts (1080 x 1080). Product photo on its studio background, product name and price,
the Maple mark bottom right. One product per post.
